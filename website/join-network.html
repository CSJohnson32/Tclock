Take Ownership of Your Career — and Earn What You're Worth

Enginuity was built on a simple idea: experienced, licensed AEC professionals should be able to run their own practice without giving up the credibility and support of an established firm.

Founded by a Professional Engineer with hands-on experience across transportation, civil infrastructure, structural design, water resources, and construction engineering, Enginuity pairs the trust clients place in a recognized firm with a compensation model built for architects, engineers, and other design professionals who want real ownership over their work and their earnings.

Most firms still run on a structure built for a different era — fixed salaries, slow promotion tracks, and a hard ceiling on what a professional can earn no matter how much value they create. For ambitious professionals, that ceiling gets frustrating fast.

You don't have to choose between stability and independence. Take on the projects you want, build your own client relationships, and keep more of what you earn — backed by Enginuity's brand, contracts, and professional liability coverage.

Our Industry Needs a Change

Licensed architects and engineers spend years earning a degree and a license, then carry real liability on every project they stamp or seal — yet compensation in much of the industry hasn't kept pace with that level of responsibility and skill.

That gap is a big part of why so many design professionals leave traditional firms within their first decade, whether that means changing industries entirely or striking out on their own.

Going independent solves the pay ceiling, but it trades one problem for another. Running a one-person firm means marketing, contracts, insurance, invoicing, and collections — work that has nothing to do with design or engineering and eats into the hours available for billable, professional work.

Enginuity exists to give AEC professionals a better trade-off: the earning upside of independence, without the administrative burden of building a firm from scratch.

How It Works

Clients bring their AEC needs to Enginuity. Projects are posted to a network job board, where licensed professionals can choose the work that fits their skills, schedule, and interests. No one is assigned work — professionals select projects they want to take on.

Enginuity handles client relationships, contracts, and professional liability insurance, so you can focus on doing great work without the overhead of running your own firm.

A Connected Network, Not Just a List of Contractors

Beyond project access, Enginuity professionals are part of a professional community. Have a design question or want a second opinion? The network is there to collaborate, discuss, and learn together — something independent consultants often miss.

Who We're Looking For

Enginuity is built on quality. To join the network, professionals must hold an active license in their discipline (PE, RA, PLS, or equivalent) and be capable of stamping or sealing their own work. All applicants go through a vetting process to ensure clients receive reliable, professional results — and that members are joining a network they can be proud of.
