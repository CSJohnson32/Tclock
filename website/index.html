Our Story

About Enginuity LLC

Enginuity LLC was built on a simple premise: credentialed engineering expertise shouldn't be hard to find or slow to access.

We created a professional network that bridges the gap between licensed Professional Engineers and the clients, contractors, and businesses who need their credentials and expertise. Whether you need a one-time stamp or an ongoing engineering partner, Enginuity makes it straightforward.

Our platform is built on trust, credential verification, and professional accountability — everything you'd expect from a network of licensed engineers operating under the standards of their state boards.
