How It Works

Getting credentialed engineering services has never been easier.

01

Tell Us About Your Project

Complete a short project request describing what you need, your timeline, and any available plans or supporting documents. Once submitted, Enginuity reviews your request and prepares it for our professional network.

02

We Match You with the Right Engineer

Your project is presented exclusively to licensed, vetted engineers within the Enginuity network who specialize in your type of work. When an engineer accepts the project under the proposed scope, schedule, and fee, they're officially assigned to your project and your service agreement is finalized.

03

Work Directly with Your Engineer

Once your project is assigned, you'll communicate directly with your engineer throughout the project. They'll answer questions, coordinate deliverables, and complete the work while following Enginuity's quality standards and standardized operating procedures.

04

Receive Your Completed Project

Your engineer delivers the completed work, whether that's stamped plans, calculations, reports, reviews, or consultation. Every project completed through Enginuity is backed by licensed professionals committed to delivering accurate, dependable engineering solutions.

Why This Model Benefits You

Instead of hiring a single engineering firm and hoping they have the right expertise or availability, Enginuity gives your project access to a network of qualified professionals. Your project is matched with an engineer who has the appropriate experience and capacity, helping reduce delays while ensuring you receive specialized expertise.

Meet the Founder

Carter Johnson, PE

Founder / Principal Engineer

Carter Johnson, PE is a licensed Professional Engineer with experience in transportation, civil infrastructure, structural design, water resources, and construction engineering. Throughout his career, Carter recognized a common challenge: finding the right expertise often meant calling multiple firms, waiting weeks for responses, or settling for whoever happened to be available — so he created Enginuity to make that process simpler.

Our Mission

To give every client direct access to the right expertise — without the limits of a single firm's capabilities — by connecting them with a growing network of licensed, vetted professionals committed to quality, responsiveness, and accountability.
