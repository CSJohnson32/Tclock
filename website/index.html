Our Story

About Enginuity LLC

Enginuity LLC started with a simple frustration: getting quality engineering work done quickly usually means choosing between a traditional firm's long queue and a freelance marketplace that doesn't know your local codes — or particularly care about getting it right.

We're building something different — a network of licensed, local Professional Engineers who follow consistent processes, so the deliverable you get is dependable no matter who's on the job. Engineers get the freedom to take projects that fit their schedule and expertise. Clients get direct access to credentialed, accountable professionals, without the overhead of a big firm or the guesswork of an anonymous platform.

It's built on trust, verified credentials, and the same standards every PE is already held to — just organized in a way that works better for everyone involved.
