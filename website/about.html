Why we exist, what we stand for, and the person behind the network.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To give every client direct access to the right expertise — without the limits of a single firm's capabilities — by connecting them with a growing network of licensed, vetted professionals committed to quality, responsiveness, and accountability.

Vision

A future where finding the right Architecture, Engineering, or Construction professional is as simple as describing the project — where clients are never limited by one firm's bandwidth or specialty, and licensed professionals have the freedom to do their best work on their own terms.

Meet the Founder

Carter Johnson, PE

Founder / Principal Engineer

Carter Johnson, PE is a licensed Professional Engineer with experience in transportation, civil infrastructure, structural design, water resources, and construction engineering. Throughout his career, Carter recognized a common challenge: finding the right expertise often meant calling multiple firms, waiting weeks for responses, or settling for whoever happened to be available — so he created Enginuity to make that process simpler.

Quality Standards

Quality Standards

Every professional in the Enginuity network is vetted before joining — license verification, reference checks, and a review of past work. Once accepted, members are held to consistent standards for scope, communication, and deliverables, so the quality you receive never depends on which professional happens to be available. It's the same rigor you'd expect from a single trusted firm, applied consistently across every discipline in our network.
