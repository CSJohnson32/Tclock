For Licensed Professional Engineers

A Better Way
to Work

The traditional engineering career path doesn't work for everyone. Heavy workloads, rigid schedules, and limited flexibility have left many talented engineers looking for something different.

Enginuity offers another option.

How It Works

Clients bring their engineering needs to Enginuity. Projects are posted to a network job board, where licensed Professional Engineers can choose the work that fits their skills, schedule, and interests. No one is assigned work — engineers select projects they want to take on.

Enginuity handles client relationships, contracts, and professional liability insurance, so engineers can focus on doing great work without the overhead of running their own firm.

A Connected Network, Not Just a List of Contractors

Beyond project access, Enginuity engineers are part of a professional community. Have a design question or want a second opinion? The network is there to collaborate, discuss, and learn together — something independent consultants often miss.

Built on Fair Compensation

Enginuity is structured so engineers are rewarded directly for the work they complete, with competitive compensation that reflects their contribution and expertise.

Who We're Looking For

Enginuity is built on quality. To join the network, engineers must hold an active Professional Engineer license and be capable of stamping their own work. All applicants go through a vetting process to ensure clients receive reliable, professional results — and that engineers are joining a network they can be proud of.
